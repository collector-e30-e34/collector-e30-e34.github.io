Parallel Lines

What the two lines share

Run the two chronologies side by side and the pattern is obvious: the 5er has always previewed what the 3er gets a few years later — multi-link rear suspension, aluminium suspension components, turbocharging, xDrive, and now electrification all landed on the 5-Series first. The E30 and E34 in this garage are themselves an example of the gap: six years apart on the factory floor, close enough that they share engine families (M20) and half their switchgear.
