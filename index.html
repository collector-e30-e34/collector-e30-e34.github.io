The Pair

Gegenüberstellung

These two cars are the same idea, drawn a decade apart. The E30 is the last of the drafting-table BMWs — a shark nose, an upright glasshouse, and not one line it doesn't need. The E34 is what happened when the wind tunnel got a vote: longer, quieter, heavier, and somehow just as honest.

This site is the garage file for both — every schematic, drivetrain, suspension layout and wheel spec, kept the way the factory kept them: as data sheets.
